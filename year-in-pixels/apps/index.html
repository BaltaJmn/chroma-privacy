What they share

All three draw one square per day, keep no streaks to protect and are made by small independent teams. Pixels and Chroma keep your days on your phone by default.

Questions

Which year in pixels app is free?

All three can be used for free. In Pixels the photos are what you pay for; in Pixa, more than two trackers and the stats; in Chroma, the year poster, the year widget and your year in sentences.

Can I keep my year in pixels on paper too?

Yes. The free 2027 printables on this site use the same grid, in A4 and US Letter, and the online maker saves your year in the browser.

Does Chroma replace a mood tracker?

No. It records what you saw, not how you felt. If you want both, a mood app and Chroma sit happily side by side.

Try Chroma for a week

Take one photo a day for seven days and look at the row of colors it leaves. If it is not for you, nothing was uploaded and there is nothing to cancel.
