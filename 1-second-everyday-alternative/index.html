A 1 Second Everyday alternative, with a photo instead of a clip

1 Second Everyday joins one second of video a day into a film of your year. Chroma keeps one photo a day and turns it into a color, so your year becomes a grid you can read at a glance.

Chroma fits if

  • You want the one-a-day habit with a photo, not a video.
  • You want your year as one still picture: 365 colors, each one with a name.
  • You would rather not subscribe: Chroma's extras cost $1.99 once.

1 Second Everyday fits if

  • You want a video diary, cut to music, of any length.
  • You want to make videos with friends, in 4K, from templates.
  • You want your clips backed up in a cloud.

Where 1 Second Everyday is the better pick

  • Moving pictures

    Sound and motion keep far more of a day than one color can. If you want to hear the day again, video wins.

  • Films you can share

    Montages of any length, with music, templates for trips, pets or a pregnancy, and videos made with friends.

  • Made for it

    Years spent on one idea, a large community and a cloud for your clips.

Questions

Is 1 Second Everyday free?

Yes, there is a free version. 1SE Pro, $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year on the US App Store, allows clips up to 10 seconds, removes the watermark and adds cloud backup, music and 4K.

Can I bring my 1SE clips into Chroma?

No. Chroma works with one photo a day and imports only its own export file.

Why a color and not the photo?

Your photos stay in Chroma, one per day. The color is what lets a whole year fit in one view: twelve months side by side, or one long gradient from winter to summer.

Try Chroma for a week

Take one photo a day for seven days and look at the row of colors it leaves. If it is not for you, nothing was uploaded and there is nothing to cancel.
