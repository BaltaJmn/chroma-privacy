A Daylio alternative where the color comes from a photo

Daylio asks how you felt and maps your moods on a Year in Pixels. Chroma asks for one photo; the day's color comes from what you saw. Here is how the two compare.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

Chroma fits if

  • You want a year in pixels without rating your mood every day.
  • You like taking photos and want each square to be something you actually saw.
  • You want the diary, the widget and your export free, with no account.

Daylio fits if

  • You want to track moods and activities and see which go together.
  • You write notes, several times a day, with templates, photos or voice notes.
  • You want goals, habits and automatic cloud backups.

Where Daylio is the better pick

  • A real mood tracker

    Your own moods, activities and stats that show what goes with your good days. Chroma has none of that, on purpose.

  • Room to write

    Notes, templates, photos and voice notes in every entry, as many entries a day as you like.

  • Years of polish

    On the App Store since 2017, more than 10 million downloads on Google Play, automatic cloud backups with Premium.

Questions

Is Daylio free?

Yes. Daylio says its free version has no limit on entries and that you can go back to it from Premium without losing data. Premium is optional; on the US App Store its in-app purchases run from $4.99 to $59.99, and the store list does not say which one is monthly, yearly or lifetime.

Can I move my Daylio entries to Chroma?

No. Chroma imports only its own export file, and its days are colors from photos, not moods, so there is nothing to convert. You can keep both: Daylio for how you felt, Chroma for what you saw.

Does Chroma track my mood?

No. You pick a color from your photo, not a feeling from a list. If a day needs it, add one word.

What is free in Chroma?

The whole diary: photos and colors, My year, sharing cards, the reminder, the today widget, Friends, the lock and export and import. Pro adds the year poster, the year widget and your year in short sentences.

Try Chroma for a week

Take one photo a day for seven days and look at the row of colors it leaves. If it is not for you, nothing was uploaded and there is nothing to cancel.
