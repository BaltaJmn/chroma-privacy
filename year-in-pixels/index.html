How to make a year in pixels

  1. Print the template

    Pick a layout and paper size above. In the print dialog, choose 100% or Actual size so the squares stay even.

  2. Choose your colors

    Decide what you are tracking and give each value a color in the key. Use pens or pencils you'll still have in December.

  3. Color one square a day

    Same time each night; just before bed works. Missed a few days? Fill them in from memory or leave them blank.

  4. Look back once a month

    After a few weeks the patterns show: runs of good weeks, how winter compares with spring.
