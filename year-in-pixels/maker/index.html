Year in Pixels maker

Pick a color in the key, then tap a day. Tap it again to clear it.

Color key

    Keyboard: arrow keys move between days, Enter paints, 1 to 9 pick a color. Ctrl or Cmd + Z undoes.

    Saved in this browser only. Clearing your browsing data erases it.

    How the maker works

    1. Your key is your brush

      Each color in the key has a meaning. Choose one, then tap days to paint them; tap a day again to clear it. Change a color and every day with it changes too.

    2. It stays in this browser

      Nothing is uploaded and there's no account. Your year is saved on this device, in this browser, until you clear the year or your browsing data.

    3. Export it as an image

      Export PNG draws your year and your key on white, ready to share or print.
