BaltaJmn App Publisher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Chroma privacy policy - FlowTime privacy policy

What this application is

BaltaJmn App Publisher is a private, single-user tool. It publishes Pins to the Pinterest business account owned by its operator, BaltaJmn, the independent developer of two mobile apps: Chroma, a year in pixels photo diary, and FlowTime, a study timer. The Pins are original images made by the operator (color palettes, year in pixels templates and study schedules) and link to the apps' websites or their App Store and Google Play pages.

It is not offered to other people, has no other users and has no sign-up.

What data it accesses

Only data belonging to the single Pinterest account that authorises it:

  • Boards: their names and identifiers, so a new Pin is filed on the right board, and the boards the application creates for these apps.
  • Pins: only those the application itself creates, to confirm a Pin was created and never create it twice.
  • Account username: read once, to confirm the application is authorised against the intended account.

It does not access, request or receive data belonging to any other Pinterest user. It reads no followers, no other person's boards or Pins and no advertising data.

What data it stores

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token, stored on the operator's own server in a file readable only by the operator. They are sent nowhere except to Pinterest and are never included in any source repository.
  • A list of the Pins the application has already created (its own image names and the Pinterest Pin identifiers), so a later run publishes new Pins rather than duplicates. It holds no personal data.

There is no database, no analytics and no third party with access to any of it.

What data it shares

None. No data obtained from Pinterest is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone.

Retention and deletion

The tokens and the Pin list can be deleted at any time by deleting the files. Revoking the application's access from the Pinterest account settings ends all access at once; no copy of Pinterest data is kept anywhere else.

Children

The application is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone other than its operator.
